Hard Times in Altdorf

    How you came to be here

    Before the first session, every character rolls on two tables. The first ties you to someone else in the crew. The second is something strange that happened to you this past year, which you still can't explain.

    1. Make someone who goes toward trouble, and who would stick with this crew once it finds them.
    2. Roll on Bonds and apply the result to the character of the player on your left. That player can accept it or make you reroll once.
    3. Roll on Something Strange. Write what you saw and what you did about it, in your own words. Don't ask the GM what it means. Your character doesn't know either.
    4. Write one thread on your sheet for each result: a name, an object, or a question.

    The Reik runs brown, the rain won't stop, and the bread costs more every week. Welcome to Altdorf.
