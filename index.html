Enemy Within

    How you came to be here

    Before the first session, every character rolls once on each table below. Bonds ties you to someone else in the crew. Tokens gives you something small you carry.

    1. Make someone who goes toward trouble, and who would stick with this crew once it finds them.
    2. Roll on Bonds and apply the result to the character of the player on your left. That player can accept it or make you reroll once.
    3. Write the thread on your sheet: a name, an object, or a question.
    4. Roll on Tokens. Decide what it means to your character and where it came from.

    The Reik runs brown, the rain won't stop, and the bread costs more every week. Welcome to Altdorf.
